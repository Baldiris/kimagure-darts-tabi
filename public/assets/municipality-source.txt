Adaptation by きまぐれダーツ旅: retain municipal ID, prefecture code and name;
remove spaces from display names; omit designated-city wards to avoid overlap
with their parent cities. Tokyo's 23 special wards remain individual choices.
Result: 1,741 choices across 47 prefectures. No live external API is required.
Regenerate with: node scripts/import-municipalities.mjs /path/to/localgovjp.json

Eligibility policy: one choice for every city, town and village in this snapshot,
plus Tokyo's 23 special wards. The six Northern Territories villages counted in
e-Stat's municipality total are not present in the source snapshot. Population,
area, tourism and transport access are not used to filter or weight choices.
The lottery is uniform across choices in the selected scope, not prefectures.

Audit (2026-10-04): 792 cities, 743 towns, 183 villages and 23 special wards.
All 47 adjusted prefecture totals match GSI's 2025-10-01 municipality counts. IDs,
names and points match this source snapshot with no duplicates or missing points.
The source still includes Hamamatsu's old seven wards after its 2024 change to
three; all 175 source ward rows are excluded, so the lottery is unaffected.
